Roadbeacon

A decentralized road event reporting system, Waze without the centralized tracking. Drivers report and see police, accidents, construction and hazards as signed Nostr events shared over relays. There is no central authority.

Open the web app Android on Zapstore

How it works

  1. Report. A driver picks a category (police, accident, road closure, construction, hazard and others). The app builds a Nostr event with the position, a geohash and an expiry time, and signs it with the driver's key. The key can be a persistent identity or an ephemeral one.
  2. Share. The signed event is published to Nostr relays. It can also travel as a compact binary message over MeshCore LoRa mesh networks (experimental, through a MeshCore Open build with the local API).
  3. See and confirm. Other drivers query relays by geohash and see the reports as markers on the map. They can confirm "still there" or deny "no longer there". Reports expire by type, for example after 1 hour for traffic jams and 30 days for speed cameras.

About the name

Roadbeacon was called Roadstr until version 0.4. It was renamed to avoid confusion with the Roadstr navigation app, which is an independent project by a different author. The two are not affiliated and not interchangeable. They share the road event format (Nostr kinds 1315 and 1316), so each can display the other's reports.
